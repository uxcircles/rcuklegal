Fees

Transparent fees, agreed before we start.

We know legal bills can be worrying, so we set out costs at the outset of every matter. Most of our work is fixed fee; where it is not, we give you regular updates. All figures exclude VAT unless stated.

How we chargeImmigrationDocumentsDebt recoveryConveyancingHourly rates

How we charge

First 30 minutes free

An initial consultation with no obligation to instruct us. Further consultations are charged at the hourly rate of the person you see.

Engagement letter

We confirm the fee, what it covers and the assumptions in writing. A retainer deposit is paid on instruction.

Balance before submission

For fixed fees, the balance is due before submission or conclusion. Ongoing cases receive interim bills.

Every case is different, so these are ranges for standard matters and are for reference only. Disbursements are not included unless agreed. The immigration fees below are for private individuals, not commercial clients.

Included

  • discussing your circumstances and confirming the right application, and any alternatives
  • advising on the Immigration Rules and whether you meet them, and how to overcome any gaps
  • reviewing your evidence and helping you obtain anything missing
  • preparing and submitting the application with a covering letter of legal submissions
  • coordinating Home Office appointments and liaising with the Home Office
  • advising you on the outcome and next steps

Not included

  • Home Office application fees, paid by you directly
  • the Immigration Health Surcharge
  • appeals if an application is refused
  • disbursements such as translation, interpreters or expert reports, which we quote before incurring

See GOV.UK for Home Office fees and the Immigration Health Surcharge.

How long it takes

We normally submit within 4 weeks of your instructions, and tell you early if it will take longer. We cannot control Home Office processing times. Estimates assume the evidence arrives on time, you meet the category requirements and there are no previous refusals or complications.

Debt recovery

Fixed fees for an undisputed business-to-business debt up to £50,000 where the debtor pays after our letter and no court proceedings are needed.

Debt value (incl. interest)Our fee (excl. VAT)Total (incl. VAT)
Up to £5,000£350£420
£5,001 – £10,000£450£540
£10,001 – £50,000£650£780

If the debt is disputed or proceedings are issued, we agree the fee with you first, usually at our hourly rates. Court fees are set by HM Courts & Tribunals Service and depend on the claim value. Undisputed matters usually take 2 to 4 weeks; if the matter goes to court, 16 to 32 weeks.

The VAT on our fee cannot be reclaimed from the debtor. Enforcement, such as bailiffs, is not included.
