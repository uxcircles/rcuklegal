Moving from Hong Kong

BN(O) visa applications for the whole family.

The BN(O) route lets British National (Overseas) status holders and their families live, work and study in the UK, and leads to settlement after five years. We check eligibility for every family member, work out the financial requirement and put the evidence together with you.

BN(O) visa guide Ask about your case
