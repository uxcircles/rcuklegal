About us

A boutique firm that explains the law in your language.

Chu & Company is a boutique law firm providing bespoke legal advice to private and commercial clients across the country. We were established in 2016 under the rules of the Solicitors Regulation Authority, with a full licence to practise as solicitors in England and Wales.

Our firm

As a multilingual firm, we explain legal processes to clients whose first language is English, Cantonese or Mandarin. Over recent years our practice has grown from immigration into related work such as agency agreements and joint ventures, from personal immigration to corporate global mobility.

We keep clients well informed so we can work together properly. We cannot remove every risk that comes with a transaction, but we can plan cost-effective measures for those risks, so there are no expectation gaps or nasty surprises.

Our dedication and attention to detail have earned us a reputation for resolving clients’ problems amicably and efficiently.

Complaints procedure

If something goes wrong

We want to hear about it, and we aim to put it right.

  1. Talk to the person handling your matter first. Most concerns can be put right quickly.
  2. If that doesn’t resolve it, write to our firm’s manager, Mr Raymond Chu. We acknowledge your complaint within 5 working days and record it in our complaints register.
  3. We will give you our final response within 8 weeks.
  4. If you are still unhappy, you can refer the matter to the Legal Ombudsman within 6 months of our final response (and within 6 years of the problem, or 3 years of when you found out about it).
